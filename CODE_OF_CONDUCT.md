# Code of conduct

BODH adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), for everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and discussions.

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good faith, and criticise ideas rather than people. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks are not tolerated, and neither is demeaning anyone for their faith, caste, gender, nationality or beliefs. Many people hold these texts sacred, and others read them in other ways; discuss interpretations with care.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Tanmay Tyagi ([@tanmaytyagii](https://github.com/tanmaytyagii)), privately. Until a contact address is listed here, open an issue with the bug report form that only asks for a private channel, without describing what happened. Behaviour that also breaks GitHub's rules can be reported to GitHub directly.

Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer decides on consequences, from a warning to a ban, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
